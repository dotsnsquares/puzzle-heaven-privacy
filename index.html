Privacy Policy

Puzzle Heaven

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Puzzle Heaven ("the Game") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Game on iOS or Android.

1. No User Account Required

The Game does not require users to create an account or provide a name, email address, password, or phone number in order to play.

2. Game Information

The Game may store game progress, preferences, settings, and other gameplay information locally on your device.

The Game does not intentionally request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone for gameplay.

3. Local Data

Game progress and preferences may be stored on your device. Uninstalling the Game may remove this information, subject to operating-system behavior and device backups.

4. Advertising

The current version of the Game does not include third-party advertising SDKs or display third-party advertisements.

Future versions may introduce advertisements. If advertising services are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the providers involved and their data practices before those features are released.

5. In-App Purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, transactions are processed through Apple's App Store or Google Play. The Game does not receive or store complete payment-card or banking information.

6. Third-Party Platform Services

Apple and Google may process technical, diagnostic, transaction, or other information in connection with their platform services, subject to their respective privacy policies.

7. Sale of Personal Information

We do not sell users' personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

The Game is designed for a general audience and does not intentionally request personal contact information from children.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored gameplay information may be removed by uninstalling the Game, subject to device backups and operating-system behavior.

For questions or requests concerning information associated with the Game, please contact us using the email address below.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the Game. No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game's features, services, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest update.
